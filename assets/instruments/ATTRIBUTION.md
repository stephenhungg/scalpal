# Instrument Asset Attribution

Scalpal instrument assets reuse selected LapGym sofa_env meshes.
Source: https://github.com/ScheiklP/sofa_env
Commit: 85bf7e05dd088b824794dda0046679df13b13e6e
Adapted: atraumatic_grasper jaws, maryland_dissector jaws, lap_scissors jaws, hook_cautery electrode, laparoscope_30 optics.
Also adapted: laparoscopic handle body, screw and lever geometry for hook, sealer, clip applier and staplers.
Source millimeters converted to meters; materials, source placement and runtime simplification adapted.
All other geometry is original generic simulator geometry.
