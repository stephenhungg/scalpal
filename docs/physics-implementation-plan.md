Implement a connected mechanics route, not unrelated demonstration widgets. Use explicit generic teaching geometry for missing volume segmentation; keep source-derived organs/IDs intact and document fidelity gaps. New wall mechanics are local/unscored until the authored case explicitly includes an incision. Skin/fat/peritoneum do not become part of the scorer merely by receiving a collider.

Use finite blade anchors already exported as `CutStart`/`CutEnd`. A spatially bounded sweep is required; an infinite plane split is not an adequate tool interaction. Discrete cut surfaces may follow tetrahedral faces at an explicit resolution, but must change topology/mechanics rather than just separate authored visual halves. Preserve total reference tissue volume/mass across pure cuts and verify this from retained-cell coverage, distinct bound corners, reconstructed rest/deformed tetrahedra and stored per-node incident mass; cached total before/after comparisons are not sufficient evidence; rebuilding vertices changes attachments and constraints consistently. Tracking loss hides/releases/freezes; retry restores initial topology/velocity/mass and fluid state. Never reset tissue to intact while leaving an advanced surgical attempt unchanged.

Material evidence must distinguish human abdominal layers, anatomical site, loading direction/rate, live/cadaver and fresh/frozen preparation. Composite indentation data are not individual tissue parameters. Literature tensile moduli alone do not establish viscosity, pressure response, anisotropy or fracture energy. Numeric fits, source licenses and error metrics belong beside the checked-in material inputs. No participant measurement/capture is started by this goal.
