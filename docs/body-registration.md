# Real Participant Body Registration

This slice implements the user's requested path: Quest passthrough image → MediaPipe shoulder/hip landmarks → acquisition-time native surface depth → generic virtual organs on a reclining participant. It automatically acquires a **generic planar torso fit**; it does not reconstruct skin, joints, internal organs or table semantics. No controller-point placement is required. Physical alignment and reclining-person accuracy remain unverified until the wearer test below passes.

## Implemented Route

`NativePresentation` defaults to AR with Unity Meta OpenXR 2.3.0 `ARCameraManager`, `ARSession` and composition layers. Meta camera-pose plugin requires Linear color space; the built-in renderer is retained. The camera clears transparent; the virtual room and mannequin are hidden. The existing floor/head/controller rig remains the source of Unity world coordinates. Right stick click changes AR/full-VR during selection/recap while preserving the pre-practice attempt; active or paused practice requires an explicit retry before a different mode. Full VR restores authored anatomy/port transforms.

`NativeBodyRegistration` starts with camera acquisition disabled, including when Android permission was previously granted. Left stick click is the operator's explicit opt-in after the participant agrees. It requests `horizonos.permission.HEADSET_CAMERA` and enables the official Meta MRUK 85 `PassthroughCameraAccess` left camera, requesting 640×480. Unsupported resolution may be negotiated by the SDK; returned dimensions are used throughout. Switching to VR, disabling the component or suspending the app stops acquisition. No participant recording/upload/export begins.

At opt-in, the app also requests `com.oculus.permission.USE_SCENE`. Both acquisition components remain off until camera and spatial grants are present and XR readiness returns. A permission-dialog pause preserves this pending request; a pause after acquisition begins still stops detection and requires a new opt-in. Denial, mode change or component disable cancels the pending request, and old denial callbacks cannot cancel a later request. Once granted, the adapter enables MRUK85 `EnvironmentRaycastManager`. Its `CustomTrackingSpace` is the same floor-space rig used by PCA/head/controllers. It uses native live environment raycasts rather than a saved room mesh; no room scan or controller-plane points are needed. Meta documents Quest3/3S support and the native provider from MRUK81. [Meta environment raycasting](https://developers.meta.com/vr/documentation/unity/unity-mr-utility-kit-environment-raycast/)

Before asynchronous image inference, the adapter caches a 17×13 raycast grid using that image's cached lens rays. It rejects frames older than 100ms at depth acquisition and aborts incomplete sampling after an 8ms main-thread budget (an individual native call cannot be preempted). App-scoped diagnostics report completion/hit count/sample duration every five seconds without image coordinates or participant data. Missing/occluded/out-of-frustum hits or unusable normals cannot supply a fit. Native `normalConfidence=1` indicates that an orientation was supplied; it is not a probability of depth accuracy. The public raycast API exposes no sensor timestamp, so this is a bounded acquisition-window approximation, **not exact image/depth sensor synchronization**. Device latency and sampling cost remain physical-test requirements.

At each observation, PCA supplies intrinsics/crop-aware rays, lens extrinsics and capture-time pose. The adapter caches these rays before HTTP inference, transforms the SDK tracking-space pose through the same `NativeWorkbench.trackingOrigin` as the head/controllers, and checks that OVR/OpenXR head spaces agree. It rejects absent/incompatible/stale camera timestamps, invalid lens rotations, and the SDK’s identity historical-head fallback using the required floor-space observer height (>0.25 m). Asynchronous GPU readback avoids Meta's documented preceding-frame behavior for blocking `Graphics.Blit`; JPEG conversion follows. No head pose sampled at response time replaces acquisition-time metadata. Image orientation still requires physical verification. [Meta PCA API](https://developers.meta.com/vr/reference/mruk/v85/class_meta_x_r_passthrough_camera_access/)

Raw JPEG travels over USB-reversed localhost port **8790** to `services/registration`. `POST /pose` echoes the exact `X-Frame-Id`, image dimensions, model hash and 33 top-left normalized image landmarks. The pinned official Pose Landmarker Lite v1 runs CPU IMAGE mode with `num_poses=2`; no detected person or more than one detected pose returns an empty invalid result. A returned single pose does not prove another person was absent. Service and Unity retain no frame files, participant landmarks or request bodies. This local detector is separate from Silas's offline motion/replay worker and Nathan's media-upload gateway.

Unity validates schema/frame ID/dimensions/model hash, unique finite landmark indices, confidence and bounds. Only shoulders **11/12** and hips **23/24** lift into the room. The SDK's bottom-left viewport receives `(x, 1-y)`. MediaPipe `z` is ignored for depth: it is relative to the hip midpoint, not metric Quest world depth. Eight interior chest/hip flank probes replace the required abdomen-center sample. They use longitudinal fractions .12/.28/.72/.88 between labeled shoulders and hips and lateral fractions .18/.82. Each probe requires four valid adjacent grid hits, consistent normals and at most50 mm deviation within its cell. At least six probes must agree within65 mm and span the upper/lower left/right torso quadrants. Local foreground/missing depth can be excluded; broad split surfaces or insufficient quadrant coverage reject the fit. Coarse-grid coverage, coherent foreground covering all probes and foreground within the rejection tolerance remain limitations. This is not a hand segmentation model. The shoulder/hip rays then intersect this automatically measured front surface. This is an external surface approximation, not recovered internal joint centers or a verified table/person classifier. [Google Pose Landmarker coordinates](https://developers.google.com/edge/mediapipe/solutions/vision/pose_landmarker/python)

## Generic Source Fit and Gates

The imported atlas frame is meters, anatomical left +X, cranial +Y, anterior −Z. A source-geometry check configures the skeletal/surface reference imports identically to the active organ builder, then checks 37 actual imported bounds/axis/metric assertions. Those reference assets do not become runtime scene dependencies. The joint reference proxies were sphere-fitted from original humerus/femur geometry, not measured from a participant.

The uniform fit uses source shoulder height **1.382441 m**, hip height **0.860876 m**, shoulder width **0.335006 m**, hip joint width **0.168614 m**, and anterior reference Z **−0.115287 m**. Shoulder-to-hip length controls one uniform scale; labeled left/right points and the independently measured anterior normal control rotation. Source front hip midpoint is translated onto the observed surface-plane hip midpoint. Organs preserve their shared source proportions. The source umbilicus `(0,1.017768,-0.115287)` supplies the fitted port frame, replacing the virtual mannequin's approximate origin in AR. A planar fit cannot measure torso thickness, internal anatomy or organ accuracy.

Four cyan markers and a render-only **42-part/110,511-triangle** organ overview allow visual inspection before practice. The scored appendectomy keeps its nine authored targets/context meshes and existing exercise rules. The larger overview does not create 42 validated surgeries; the greater omentum is omitted from the overview to avoid covering the other abdominal organs and consuming 77,968 extra triangles. It remains in the scored exercise.

Three stable observations automatically acquire the fit and show the organ preview. **B** confirms the case, not calibration. Scoring, coach tracking, anatomy/port visibility and resume commands require the same current accepted fit in AR. Before acceptance, three uninterrupted successful observations are required. After acceptance, at most two consecutive temporary no-pose/occlusion/depth/readback/HTTP failures retain the unchanged measured fit, bounded by **2 s from the last successful image acquisition**. The third failure or expiry hides anatomy and closes scoring; no failed response renews this clock. Wrong frame/model, malformed points, multiple poses, incompatible timestamps, implausible geometry and XR loss invalidate immediately. Replies older than **1.25 s at processing** are rejected. Capture-to-result latency is logged every five seconds without participant coordinates; the two-second fit budget supports the tested600 ms serialized pipeline, not arbitrary network latency. Accepted anatomy remains fixed; participant movement beyond **35 mm**, **5°** or a **0.05 scale** delta invalidates it and automatically reacquires three stable observations. The source-point residual allowance of **90 mm** is an engineering rejection bound for this approximate correspondence, **not a demonstrated anatomical accuracy**. Retry invalidates acceptance; changing presentation stops the camera and invalidates the previous fit while preserving a pre-practice attempt. A different mode during active or paused practice requires an explicit retry; see the [mode API](native-session.md#operating-room-mode-api). Virtual effects still use authored exercise contacts rather than arbitrary tissue slicing.

## Run and Verify

1. In `services/registration`: `uv sync --frozen`, then `uv run --frozen scalpal-registration fetch-model`, then `uv run --frozen scalpal-registration serve`.
2. Configure the development player using `scripts/quest/configure_session.py`; this restores coach/Spacetime routes plus localhost8790 and does not start camera capture.
3. Run `scripts/quest/verify_session.py --suite registration` and `--suite unity`. Model download is explicit and separate from serving. The cached model is Git-ignored.
4. Build with `NativeSessionBuild.Build`, which runs the scene/body/input/session verification before producing the APK.

The permission-lifecycle revision passed **76 synthetic native surface/projection/automatic-acquisition/permission checks**, the existing 160 instrument/24 input/75 playthrough/72 attempt/68 coach checks, and 33 production-bridge boundary checks through `verify_session.py --suite unity`. `--suite registration` passed **33 tests**, including actual blank-JPEG CPU inference and loopback HTTP; positive/multiple-person fixtures are synthetic. The earlier `0.3.1-auto-body` APK built and installed over USB with camera and spatial permissions; the `0.3.2-auto-body` permission revision also built and installed over USB (code 7). The earlier scene also passed a second verification after preserving its existing serialized identities. The latest code-7 launch passed actual app-scoped XR/head/floor/focus smoke. Both controllers were untracked and acquisition remained off, so automatic acquisition/sampling timing and participant alignment are still unverified. Synthetic grid/projection tests and blank-image service tests do not prove reclining-person accuracy, depth coverage or Quest frame time.

For the wearer test: launch without acquisition; verify the real room is visible and mannequin hidden. With an agreed reclining participant in good light, press the left stick once to enable detection and grant camera/spatial permissions. Keep both shoulders and hips visible; the markers and 42-part organ preview should align automatically. Check both eyes, then move the headset without moving the participant: markers must remain on the same external locations. Move the participant or cover a shoulder: patient organs/ports/effects and scoring must pause, then recover automatically after three stable observations. Check refresh/frame time, source-image orientation, acquisition-grid timing and actual inference latency. A synthetic editor surface or blank image cannot substitute for this test.

The standalone instrument bench patches retain their authored XR-only interaction gate for workspace testing; they are not fitted patient organs and do not score case progress. The accepted body fit gates the patient anatomy, ports and scored contact path. Arbitrary deformable-organ cutting remains unimplemented.
