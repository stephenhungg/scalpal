description: "What's been measured, and what hasn't."
---

We try to keep this honest. "Measured" means someone ran it and saw the result. Everything else is built but unverified.

## Measured

- **Quest:** native camera access, Unity deployment, controller tracking and held tools on a physical Quest 3S. Tool motion lag was reported and fixed.
- **FinchNode:** a patient consented on FinchNode's hosted page and their case read end to end.
- **Presage:** a live iPhone run got a stable pulse (85 bpm, 98% confidence) and breathing (17.6 per minute), and captured a measured baseline.
- **Motion:** a 20 s sample webcam clip tracked 621 of 621 frames and went through the real gateway and SpacetimeDB to a viewer download.
- **Dashboard:** live video between two browser contexts at 1280×720 and about 30 fps, on one machine.

## Not yet verified

- A full physical playthrough on the headset, start to finish.
- Fitting anatomy to a real person in mixed reality.
- Motion reconstruction from a real Quest passthrough clip.
- Vision on real Quest frames or real surgical instruments.
- Vitals inside the headset. Today they show in the dashboard; Unity doesn't read them yet.
- Dashboard video across networks through TURN.

## Always true

- Replay is recorded motion played back on a simulated hand, not a learned robot policy.
- The anatomy is generic teaching anatomy, not the volunteer's organs.
- Presage readings are wellness information, not a diagnosis.
- Synthetic and demo data is labeled wherever it appears.
